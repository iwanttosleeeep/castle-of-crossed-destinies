# BaZi Knowledge Boundary

This is a compact interpretive lexicon, not a chart calculator. Use only facts
already supplied by the visitor.

## Structural rules

- `BAZI-STRUCT-001` — A BaZi chart is represented by year, month, day, and hour
  pillars, each containing a Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch. The Day Stem is the
  Day Master and the reference point for relational labels.
- `BAZI-TIME-001` — The hour pillar and some luck-cycle conventions depend on birth
  time and school-specific calculation choices. Missing or approximate time limits
  any claim using them.
- `BAZI-SEASON-001` — Month branch and seasonal context materially condition an
  element's expression. Never label a Day Master strong or weak from element counts
  alone.
- `BAZI-WHOLE-001` — Interpret a reported structure, useful element, or favorable
  element only if the dossier names it and its source convention. Do not recreate it.

## Five-Phase and Ten-God rules

- `BAZI-PHASE-001` — The producing cycle is Wood→Fire→Earth→Metal→Water→Wood;
  the controlling cycle is Wood→Earth→Water→Fire→Metal→Wood. Describe these as
  symbolic functional relationships, not physical causes.
- `BAZI-PEER-001` — 比肩 and 劫財 belong to the companion/peer family. In a
  supported context they may frame autonomy, comparison, cooperation, or competition;
  they do not prove siblings, rivalry, or financial loss.
- `BAZI-OUTPUT-001` — 食神 and 傷官 belong to the output family. They may frame
  production, expression, craft, critique, or release; they do not by themselves
  prove talent, rebellion, or a career.
- `BAZI-WEALTH-001` — 正財 and 偏財 are what the Day Master controls within the
  system. They may frame management of results, exchange, or material priorities;
  they never guarantee income, windfalls, or a spouse.
- `BAZI-POWER-001` — 正官 and 七殺 are what controls the Day Master. They may frame
  standards, duty, authority, constraint, urgency, or pressure; they do not prove
  status, danger, a husband, or punishment.
- `BAZI-RESOURCE-001` — 正印 and 偏印 belong to the resource family. They may frame
  learning, support, absorption, authorization, or unconventional inputs; they do
  not prove education level, a mother figure, or protection.

## Interaction and timing rules

- `BAZI-INTERACT-001` — A supplied combination, clash, harm, punishment, or break
  describes a relationship between named stems or branches. Discuss tension or
  coordination only in the domains supported by other facts.
- `BAZI-TRANSFORM-001` — A reported combination does not automatically transform
  into another phase. Require the source report to state that transformation and
  its convention.
- `BAZI-CYCLE-001` — Interpret 大運 or 流年 only when the exact pillar and active
  date range are supplied. Frame it as a temporary symbolic emphasis, not a promised
  event.
- `BAZI-GENDER-001` — Traditional spouse/parent/gender correspondences are historical
  conventions. Translate them into role or relationship dynamics only when the user
  requests that lens; never infer gender, sexuality, or family outcome.
