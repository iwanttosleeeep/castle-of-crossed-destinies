# Shared Theme Taxonomy

Use only these theme IDs so the Tribunal can compare unlike systems without
pretending their concepts are identical.

- `identity_orientation`: self-concept, role, visibility, or personal direction.
- `decision_style`: how choices, initiative, restraint, or response are framed.
- `thinking_communication`: learning, language, analysis, expression, or inquiry.
- `relationships_boundaries`: reciprocity, attachment, cooperation, conflict, or limits.
- `work_creation`: craft, contribution, ambition, creativity, or public activity.
- `resources_stewardship`: money symbolism, material priorities, maintenance, or allocation.
- `stress_adaptation`: pressure, vulnerability, coping, disruption, or recovery.
- `change_timing`: a supplied cycle or time-bounded symbolic emphasis.
- `meaning_imagination`: belief, reflection, symbolism, intuition, or worldview.

Do not force a claim into a theme merely to make systems agree. Use no claim when
the mapping would erase an important difference.
