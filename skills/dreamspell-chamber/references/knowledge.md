# Dreamspell Knowledge Boundary

Dreamspell is a modern esoteric calendar/symbol system developed by José and Lloydine
Argüelles and released around 1990. It draws inspiration from Maya calendrics but is
not interchangeable with historical or living Maya day-count traditions.

## Structure

- `DREAM-PROVENANCE-001` — Name Dreamspell's modern provenance and avoid “Mayan”
  authenticity claims.
- `DREAM-STRUCT-001` — The system combines 13 Galactic Tones and 20 Solar Seals into
  260 Galactic Signatures/Kin. Treat the combination as symbolic structure.
- `DREAM-WAVESPELL-001` — A Wavespell is a 13-position process. Use position only
  when supplied; do not infer it from a Kin number.
- `DREAM-ORACLE-001` — Guide, Analog, Antipode, and Occult are symbolic relations.
  Describe support, contrast, or reflection without turning them into people or events.
- `DREAM-CASTLE-001` — The five Castles organize groups of Wavespells. They are
  contextual labels, not status levels.

## Thirteen Tone lexicon

- `DREAM-TONE-1` — Magnetic: orient/unify around purpose.
- `DREAM-TONE-2` — Lunar: identify polarity, friction, or challenge.
- `DREAM-TONE-3` — Electric: activate through service or connection.
- `DREAM-TONE-4` — Self-Existing: define form, measure, or boundary.
- `DREAM-TONE-5` — Overtone: gather agency, radiance, or command.
- `DREAM-TONE-6` — Rhythmic: organize balance and sustainable cadence.
- `DREAM-TONE-7` — Resonant: attune, listen, or channel attention.
- `DREAM-TONE-8` — Galactic: align conduct with stated integrity.
- `DREAM-TONE-9` — Solar: focus and pulse intention.
- `DREAM-TONE-10` — Planetary: produce, embody, or complete a form.
- `DREAM-TONE-11` — Spectral: release, dissolve, or liberate.
- `DREAM-TONE-12` — Crystal: cooperate, share, and dedicate.
- `DREAM-TONE-13` — Cosmic: endure, remain present, and transcend a cycle.

## Twenty Seal lexicon

- `DREAM-SEAL-DRAGON` — nurture, beginning, or conditions for emergence.
- `DREAM-SEAL-WIND` — breath, communication, or animating exchange.
- `DREAM-SEAL-NIGHT` — dream, inward abundance, or incubation.
- `DREAM-SEAL-SEED` — targeting, growth conditions, or flowering.
- `DREAM-SEAL-SERPENT` — instinct, vitality, or embodied survival.
- `DREAM-SEAL-WORLDBRIDGER` — transition, equalization, or letting go; “death” is
  metaphorical unless independently discussed outside this system.
- `DREAM-SEAL-HAND` — knowing through doing, repair, or completion.
- `DREAM-SEAL-STAR` — pattern, art, elegance, or harmony.
- `DREAM-SEAL-MOON` — flow, purification, or emotional movement.
- `DREAM-SEAL-DOG` — loyalty, care, or relational heart.
- `DREAM-SEAL-MONKEY` — play, improvisation, or perceptual flexibility.
- `DREAM-SEAL-HUMAN` — choice, influence, or reflective wisdom.
- `DREAM-SEAL-SKYWALKER` — exploration, wakefulness, or crossing boundaries.
- `DREAM-SEAL-WIZARD` — receptivity, patience, or timeless attention; not literal magic.
- `DREAM-SEAL-EAGLE` — vision, perspective, or mental overview.
- `DREAM-SEAL-WARRIOR` — inquiry, courage, or intelligent questioning.
- `DREAM-SEAL-EARTH` — navigation, orientation, or noticed synchronicity.
- `DREAM-SEAL-MIRROR` — reflection, order, or clarifying boundaries.
- `DREAM-SEAL-STORM` — catalysis, renewal, or self-generated change.
- `DREAM-SEAL-SUN` — illumination, presence, or life-giving warmth.

## Synthesis rules

- `DREAM-SYNTH-001` — A Tone describes how a process moves; a Seal describes the
  symbolic action or field. A combined claim must cite both facts.
- `DREAM-SYNCH-001` — Synchronicity is an interpretive practice of noticing patterns,
  not proof of cosmic causation.
