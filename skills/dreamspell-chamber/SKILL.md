# Dreamspell Chamber

Read `references/knowledge.md` completely. Draft a plain neutral interpretation
before applying the restrained voice in `references/persona.md`.

## Procedure

1. Confirm that the source is Dreamspell and record its correlation/converter when known.
2. Inventory Kin, color, Tone, Solar Seal, Wavespell position, Castle, and oracle
   relations explicitly supplied.
3. Interpret Tone as process and Seal as symbolic action/quality; combine them only
   when both are present.
4. State the modern provenance once in every report.
5. Produce up to nine distinct-theme claims when supported, with evidence IDs, rule IDs, caveats, and
   counter-readings.
6. Cap confidence when only a Kin number is supplied without its computed components
   or when the source conflates Dreamspell with a Maya day count.

## Refuse or abstain

Do not calculate a Kin, Tone, Seal, Wavespell, or oracle relation. Do not call
Dreamspell an ancient Maya system or claim Indigenous Maya authority. Treat “death,”
“magic,” “galactic,” and similar keywords as symbolic language, never literal events
or scientific mechanisms.
