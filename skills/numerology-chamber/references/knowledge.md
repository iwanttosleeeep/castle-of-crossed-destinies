# Numerology Knowledge Boundary

Numerology traditions use different letter mappings, source names, and reduction
rules. Treat the report's result as the fact; this chamber does not calculate it.

## Convention and calculation roles

- `NUM-CONVENTION-001` — Pythagorean and Chaldean mappings are not interchangeable.
  Require a named system for name-derived values.
- `NUM-NAME-001` — Name spelling, transliteration, birth name versus current name,
  treatment of Y, and inclusion of middle names are material. Do not normalize them.
- `NUM-LIFEPATH-001` — A supplied Life Path number is conventionally read as broad
  recurring orientation or lessons derived from birth date, not a fixed destiny.
- `NUM-EXPRESSION-001` — A supplied Expression/Destiny number is read as capacities
  or modes of expression derived from the relevant full name.
- `NUM-SOUL-001` — A supplied Soul Urge/Heart's Desire number is read as motivations
  within systems that derive it from vowels.
- `NUM-PERSONALITY-001` — A supplied Personality number is read as outward
  presentation within systems that derive it from consonants.
- `NUM-BIRTHDAY-001` — A supplied Birthday number is a narrower accent than Life Path.
- `NUM-CYCLE-001` — Personal Year/Month/Day numbers require the target period and
  source reduction method. Treat them as temporary symbolic themes.
- `NUM-MASTER-001` — Preserve 11, 22, or 33 as master numbers only when the report
  does. Also retain the reduced base (2, 4, or 6) when supplied; “master” means
  intensified tension/potential, not superiority.

## Number lexicon

Each rule contains a potential and an excess. Never report only the flattering side.

- `NUM-VALUE-1` — initiative and differentiation; excess: isolation or overcontrol.
- `NUM-VALUE-2` — cooperation and sensitivity; excess: dependence or indecision.
- `NUM-VALUE-3` — expression and play; excess: diffusion or performance without depth.
- `NUM-VALUE-4` — structure and persistence; excess: rigidity or fear of disruption.
- `NUM-VALUE-5` — movement and experimentation; excess: restlessness or inconsistency.
- `NUM-VALUE-6` — care and responsibility; excess: overfunctioning or intrusion.
- `NUM-VALUE-7` — analysis and inward inquiry; excess: withdrawal or distrust.
- `NUM-VALUE-8` — organization and stewardship of power/resources; excess: domination
  or reducing value to achievement.
- `NUM-VALUE-9` — synthesis and broad concern; excess: blurred boundaries or
  difficulty completing.
- `NUM-VALUE-11` — heightened ideation/sensitivity joined to the base 2; excess:
  overstimulation or unstable idealization.
- `NUM-VALUE-22` — large-scale structuring joined to the base 4; excess: pressure,
  rigidity, or grand plans without pacing.
- `NUM-VALUE-33` — service/communication joined to the base 6; excess:
  self-sacrifice, rescue dynamics, or moral burden.
