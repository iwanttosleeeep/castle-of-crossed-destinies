# Zi Wei Dou Shu Knowledge Boundary

Zi Wei Dou Shu uses symbolic “stars” arranged across twelve life-domain palaces.
These are chart symbols, not necessarily astronomical bodies. Treat school and layer
differences as material.

## Structural rules

- `ZIWEI-STRUCT-001` — Interpret the twelve palaces as domains. 命宮 frames overall
  orientation; 兄弟 peers; 夫妻 partnership; 子女 children/creation; 財帛 resources;
  疾厄 constitution/stress; 遷移 external environment; 交友 networks; 官祿 public
  work; 田宅 home/assets; 福德 inner life; 父母 parents/institutions.
- `ZIWEI-BODY-001` — 身宮 is a supplementary emphasis only when its location is
  explicitly supplied. Do not treat it as a second independent identity.
- `ZIWEI-SYNTH-001` — A palace reading requires at minimum the palace plus a supplied
  principal star or explicit empty-palace statement. Auxiliary stars and 四化 modify
  rather than replace that base.
- `ZIWEI-SCHOOL-001` — Schools differ in emphasis and some 四化 assignments. Preserve
  the source school's table; if unknown, discuss only transformations explicitly listed.
- `ZIWEI-TRIAD-001` — Use 三方四正 only when the relevant linked palaces or report
  synthesis are supplied. Never reconstruct them from memory.

## Four Transformations

- `ZIWEI-LU-001` — 化祿 may frame attraction, access, increase, or ease in the named
  palace; it does not guarantee money or luck.
- `ZIWEI-QUAN-001` — 化權 may frame agency, responsibility, control, or pressure; it
  does not prove authority or promotion.
- `ZIWEI-KE-001` — 化科 may frame visibility, ordering, reputation, or articulation;
  it does not guarantee credentials or fame.
- `ZIWEI-JI-001` — 化忌 may frame friction, fixation, debt of attention, or repeated
  work; it is not a curse and does not prove loss.

## Fourteen-major-star lexicon

Use these as bounded archetypal verbs, not personality verdicts:

- `ZIWEI-STAR-ZW` — 紫微: organize, centralize, coordinate, carry responsibility.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TJ` — 天機: plan, adapt, calculate, revise.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TY` — 太陽: illuminate, contribute, become visible.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-WQ` — 武曲: execute, measure, allocate, sustain.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TT` — 天同: reconcile, simplify, seek ease.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-LZ` — 廉貞: negotiate desire, rules, boundaries, or institutions.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TF` — 天府: store, administer, preserve, steward.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TYIN` — 太陰: reflect, contain, care, accumulate.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TL` — 貪狼: explore appetite, sociability, variety, or performance.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-JM` — 巨門: question, explain, dispute, uncover.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TX` — 天相: mediate, compare, support standards.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-TLIANG` — 天梁: protect, advise, uphold principle.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-QS` — 七殺: decide under pressure, cut through, assume risk.
- `ZIWEI-STAR-PJ` — 破軍: disrupt, dismantle, rebuild, reallocate.

## Timing

- `ZIWEI-CYCLE-001` — Keep natal, 大限, and 流年 testimony separate. A cycle can
  emphasize an already supported theme for a stated period; it cannot promise an event.
