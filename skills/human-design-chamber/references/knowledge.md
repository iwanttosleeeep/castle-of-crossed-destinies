# Human Design Knowledge Boundary

Human Design is a modern system associated with Ra Uru Hu's teachings beginning in
1987. Treat its mechanics as internal symbolic claims and behavioral experiments,
not validated biology or physics.

## Priority and structure

- `HD-ORIGIN-001` — Label Human Design as a modern self-reflection system. Do not
  present its origin story or claimed mechanisms as scientific evidence.
- `HD-PRIORITY-001` — Type, Strategy, and Authority are the primary interpretive
  layer. Profile, Centers, Channels, and Gates refine but do not override it.
- `HD-SOURCE-001` — A BodyGraph depends on birth data and calculation software.
  Require a supplied chart source and time precision; never reconstruct it.

## Type and Strategy

- `HD-TYPE-GENERATOR` — Generator (including Manifesting Generator): Strategy is
  waiting to respond. Frame this as noticing available response before commitment,
  not passivity.
- `HD-TYPE-MANIFESTOR` — Manifestor: Strategy is informing affected people before
  initiating. This is a communication experiment, not permission-seeking.
- `HD-TYPE-PROJECTOR` — Projector: Strategy is waiting for recognition/invitation
  in major relational or directional matters. Do not universalize it to every action.
- `HD-TYPE-REFLECTOR` — Reflector: the traditional experiment is allowing roughly a
  lunar cycle for major decisions. Do not turn this into a compulsory delay.

## Authority

- `HD-AUTH-EMOTIONAL` — Emotional Authority: allow time across an emotional wave;
  avoid claiming certainty in an immediate high or low.
- `HD-AUTH-SACRAL` — Sacral Authority: observe immediate embodied response to
  something concrete; do not equate it with every intuition.
- `HD-AUTH-SPLENIC` — Splenic Authority: notice quiet, momentary instinctive
  information; avoid retrospective certainty.
- `HD-AUTH-EGO` — Ego/Heart Authority: hear what one is genuinely willing to commit
  resources or will toward; do not glorify willpower.
- `HD-AUTH-SELF` — Self-Projected Authority: use speaking and hearing one's own
  direction as a sounding process.
- `HD-AUTH-MENTAL` — Environmental/Mental Projector process: use trusted sounding
  boards and appropriate environments; other people are not decision-makers.
- `HD-AUTH-LUNAR` — Lunar Authority: observe a major question through a lunar cycle
  and changing contexts; it is not divination by a single lunar event.

## Secondary layers

- `HD-CENTER-001` — A defined Center is described within the system as consistent;
  an undefined/open Center as variable and receptive to conditioning. Neither is
  better, worse, empty, or broken.
- `HD-CHANNEL-001` — A Channel requires both activated Gates connecting two Centers.
  Do not infer a Channel from one hanging Gate.
- `HD-DEFINITION-001` — Single, Split, Triple-Split, and Quadruple-Split describe
  connectivity among defined Centers, not psychological integration or pathology.
- `HD-PROFILE-001` — Interpret both Profile lines together and only when supplied:
  1 investigates foundations; 2 alternates natural ability and retreat; 3 learns
  through trial/adaptation; 4 works through networks; 5 attracts projection and
  practical expectations; 6 moves through observation and role-model themes.
- `HD-GATE-001` — A Gate or Line is a narrow modifier. Require its exact number and
  line; do not build a life verdict from one activation.
- `HD-EXPERIMENT-001` — End practical testimony with an observable, reversible
  experiment. Never tell the visitor to abandon medical, financial, legal, or
  relationship judgment in favor of Strategy/Authority.
