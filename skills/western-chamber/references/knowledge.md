# Western Astrology Knowledge Boundary

Use this lexicon for bounded symbolic interpretation. Keep modern psychological and
traditional condition-based techniques distinct when the report identifies them.

## Grammar and convention

- `WEST-GRAMMAR-001` — Planet=function, sign=manner, house=life domain,
  aspect=relationship between functions. A sound synthesis names each component.
- `WEST-CONVENTION-001` — Tropical/sidereal zodiac and house system are material
  metadata. Never silently choose a convention.
- `WEST-TIME-001` — Ascendant, Midheaven, houses, and often the Moon are sensitive
  to birth-time precision. Unknown time sharply limits related claims.
- `WEST-HOUSE-SIGN-001` — Signs and houses are not interchangeable. Reject the
  “twelve-letter alphabet” shortcut unless the source explicitly uses it, and label it.

## Planet and angle functions

- `WEST-PLANET-SUN` — Sun: organizing identity, vitality, visibility, authorship.
- `WEST-PLANET-MOON` — Moon: needs, habits, memory, regulation, belonging.
- `WEST-PLANET-MERCURY` — Mercury: perception, language, learning, exchange.
- `WEST-PLANET-VENUS` — Venus: valuation, attraction, relating, aesthetic preference.
- `WEST-PLANET-MARS` — Mars: assertion, effort, separation, conflict, pursuit.
- `WEST-PLANET-JUPITER` — Jupiter: expansion, confidence, meaning, synthesis.
- `WEST-PLANET-SATURN` — Saturn: limits, duty, duration, structure, consolidation.
- `WEST-PLANET-URANUS` — Uranus: discontinuity, independence, experimentation.
- `WEST-PLANET-NEPTUNE` — Neptune: imagination, permeability, idealization, diffusion.
- `WEST-PLANET-PLUTO` — Pluto: intensification, power, compulsion, renewal.
- `WEST-ANGLE-001` — Ascendant frames approach and embodiment; MC public direction;
  Descendant encounter/partnership; IC roots/private foundation. Require supplied degrees
  or placements and adequate time precision.

## Sign qualifiers

- `WEST-ELEMENT-001` — Fire signs emphasize activation; Earth materialization; Air
  conceptual/social exchange; Water affective/receptive processes.
- `WEST-MODALITY-001` — Cardinal signs initiate, Fixed signs stabilize, Mutable signs
  adapt. These are modifiers, not personality diagnoses.

## House domains

- `WEST-HOUSE-001` — 1 self/approach; 2 resources/values; 3 learning/local exchange;
  4 home/roots; 5 creation/pleasure; 6 routine/labor/maintenance; 7 partnership;
  8 shared resources/dependency; 9 worldview/higher study/long journeys; 10 public
  role/vocation; 11 allies/groups/hopes; 12 withdrawal/hidden conditions/institutions.

## Aspect and condition rules

- `WEST-ASPECT-CONJ` — Conjunction combines and intensifies two functions; quality
  depends on planets, sign, house, orb, and wider context.
- `WEST-ASPECT-OPP` — Opposition frames polarity, negotiation, projection, or balance.
- `WEST-ASPECT-SQUARE` — Square frames friction that can provoke action or repeated work.
- `WEST-ASPECT-TRINE` — Trine frames ease or fluency, which may support talent or inertia.
- `WEST-ASPECT-SEXTILE` — Sextile frames available cooperation that often requires use.
- `WEST-ASPECT-ORB` — Do not call an aspect dominant without a supplied orb or explicit
  source ranking.
- `WEST-DIGNITY-001` — Use domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall, sect, angularity, or
  reception only when explicitly supplied and within the source framework. Condition
  modifies expression; it is not moral worth.
- `WEST-TRANSIT-001` — A transit is a temporary symbolic contact. Require transiting
  body, natal target, aspect, and date window; never promise a concrete event.
